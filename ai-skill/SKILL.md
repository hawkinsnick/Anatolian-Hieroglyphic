# Anatolian Hieroglyphic Research AI

Use this corpus as an evidence router, not a decipherment oracle. Preserve source attribution, edition dependence, uncertainty and period distinctions. Never infer Aegean/Anatolian sign identity or linguistic relationship from visual similarity. Digital derivatives are not independent witnesses. Consult `research/pre-expert-maximum.json` before proposing expert review.

For version 2.0.0 consult `research/digital-entry-dossiers.json` and `analysis/current-status.json`. Use stable eDiAna IDs. Reference fields contain bibliography, internal cross-references and source notices: keep their types separate. Period candidates are secondary label matches, not adjudicated dates. Preserve all conflicting candidates. Unknown object class never means monumental. Do not turn 287 digital entries into a physical-object count or promote metadata to expert review or readings.
