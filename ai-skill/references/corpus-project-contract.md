# Corpus Project AI Research Contract — 0.3

The repository corpus is authoritative. The AI layer is a rebuildable research interface, not a second corpus.

## Evidence contract
Every substantive answer should distinguish: observation/source record; normalized or derived representation; computational result; scholarly interpretation; AI-derived analysis. Missing evidence is reported as unknown or blocked, never filled by inference.

## Provenance and citation
Prefer stable record/claim identifiers. Cite repository paths and source locators when present. Preserve evidence hashes and source lineage. Multiple digital copies of one upstream witness are not independent witnesses.

## Rights
Consult the indexed rights/NOTICE artifacts before reproducing source-derived material. Transformation does not broaden upstream rights. When redistribution is unclear, cite or describe the source rather than reproducing protected content.

## Cross-corpus safety
Interoperability permits comparison, not equivalence. Similar signs, strings, positions, or statistics do not by themselves establish common values, language relationship, borrowing, morphology, decipherment, or independent replication.

## Staleness
Read `ai-skill/generated/fleet-contract-index.json` and replay `python ai-skill/scripts/fleet_contract.py` against the current checkout. Every declared authority and native bundle input must match its recorded SHA-256. On mismatch report the bundle as stale and use canonical files until rebuilt. Hash consistency does not establish epigraphic truth.
